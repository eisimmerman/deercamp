SECURITY

INDEXES

- Production contains those indexes plus one feedItems composite index.
- Active DeerCamp feedItems queries filter campId and published but do not order by createdAt.
- Feed sorting occurs in application code.
- The additional production feedItems composite index is therefore classified as a legacy/stale but harmless index.
- No production index deletion was performed during this audit.
